# Start here

## 1. Add your key (once)

1. Open github.com/settings/codespaces in a new tab.
2. Next to "Secrets", click **New secret**.
3. Name: `OPENROUTER_API_KEY`
4. Value: your key from the welcome email (starts with `sk-or-`).
5. Repository access: pick this repository. Click **Add secret**.
6. Come back here. A box at the bottom right asks to reload. Click **Reload to apply**.

## 2. Start Claude Code

1. Open the terminal: menu (three lines, top left) > Terminal > New Terminal.
2. Type `claude` and press Enter.
3. Paste the prompt from your hackathon page and press Enter.

## 3. Turn on your page (once)

1. Open your repository on github.com (the page you made the codespace from).
2. Click **Settings**, then **Pages** on the left.
3. Under **Branch**, pick **main** and **/ (root)**. Click **Save**.
4. Wait 1 minute. Your page is at `https://<your GitHub name>.github.io/<repository name>/`

Claude Code saves your work to GitHub. After step 3, every save updates your page.

## If something goes wrong

Ask the AI Shift Hackathon Assistant. Copy the last lines from the terminal into the chat.
